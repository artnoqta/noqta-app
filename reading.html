Passage 1: Urban Agriculture and Sustainability

Urban agriculture refers to the practice of cultivating, processing, and distributing food in or around urban areas. Unlike traditional rural farming, urban agriculture integrates directly into the economic and ecological systems of cities. Vertical farming, rooftop gardens, and community plots are increasingly recognized as viable solutions to food insecurity and urban heat island effects. Research indicates that localized food production significantly reduces carbon emissions associated with long-distance food transport.
